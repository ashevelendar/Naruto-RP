### History
Ashe left home at 16 because she wanted to explore the world[cite: 1]. During her travels she became involved in rescuing people from a trafficking operation[cite: 1]. The rescued people became the foundation of Riverhome[cite: 1].

Over time she helped establish housing, agriculture, trade, council government, charter, guard, schoolhouse, apprenticeships, infrastructure, and livestock[cite: 1].

### Kōton History
Her parents tried to train her when she was young, but as Kōton is truly unique, no one knew WHAT it was[cite: 1]. Most of Ashe's actual Kōton development came through experimentation[cite: 1]. The Hollow became her private training and research facility[cite: 1].
