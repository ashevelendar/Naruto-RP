# 2. KNOWLEDGE BOUNDARY

The most important rule:

> **The player knowing something does not mean Ashe knows it.**

Every important fact should have an in-world source before Ashe can act upon it as known information.

The wider Naruto canon is background reality, not automatic player knowledge.

Do not give Ashe:
- Future events
- Future character development
- Secret plans
- Hidden identities
- Exact canon timelines unless learned in-world
- Names of people she has never encountered
- Information that only the audience would know

---

# 3. ASHE'S FATHER: KNOWLEDGE BOUNDARY

Ashe's father is a retired Konoha shinobi.

Therefore it is reasonable for him to know things a former Konoha shinobi would genuinely know.

This includes:
- Konoha shinobi practices
- Konoha's military culture
- Shinobi combat
- Taijutsu
- Chakra fundamentals
- The Nine-Tails / Demon Fox incident
- The general fear surrounding the Nine-Tails
- General knowledge of Konoha's shinobi institutions
- The Chūnin Exams as a shinobi institution

However, retirement and distance matter.

He should **not** automatically know:
- Current Konoha intelligence
- Current Genin rosters
- Private Konoha plans
- Sand's secret plans
- Orochimaru's plans
- Upcoming attacks
- Future canon events
- Information obtained after his retirement unless it reached him through a plausible source

He is a retired shinobi living in a small settlement away from Konoha, not a current intelligence officer.

---

# 4. ASHE'S KNOWLEDGE OF CANON CHARACTERS

## Naruto Uzumaki

Do not assume Ashe knows Naruto's name simply because the audience does.

Her father can know about the Nine-Tails / Demon Fox and the fear surrounding it.

Whether Ashe personally knows that Naruto is the jinchūriki must be established through an in-world event or conversation.

Before that happens, she should not casually refer to Naruto as the Nine-Tails' host.

Naruto's future importance is unknown.

---

## Sakura Haruno

Ashe has no established reason to know Sakura personally.

Do not casually introduce Sakura's name into Ashe's knowledge simply because she is a canon character.

If Sakura becomes relevant, Ashe should learn about her through:
- Meeting her
- Hearing her name
- A report
- Her parents
- Another shinobi
- Another plausible in-world source

---

## Sasuke Uchiha

The Uchiha name is historically significant.

Ashe may reasonably know that the Uchiha were an important Konoha clan if that knowledge fits her background.

That does **not** mean Ashe automatically knows:
- Sasuke's current activities
- Sasuke's current team
- Sasuke's participation in the Chūnin Exams
- His abilities
- His personality
- His future

But the following are **not automatically known**:

- Exact schedule

- Political plots

# 18. CONTINUITY PRIORITY

When information conflicts, use this order:

1. **Most recent explicit player statement**
2. **Most recent established roleplay event**
3. **Current State card**
4. **Specific Character / Location / Ability card**
5. **Historical Timeline**
6. **General Naruto canon knowledge**
7. **Inference**

Never use general canon knowledge to override something explicitly established in the roleplay.

Never use inference to silently replace an unknown.
