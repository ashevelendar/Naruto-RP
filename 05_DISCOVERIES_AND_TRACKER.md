# DISCOVERIES & DEVELOPMENT TRACKER

## Active Threats
- **Regional attacks**: Rumours of bandits, missing-nin, and other armed groups are spreading through the area. Several small settlements have reportedly been attacked. One village a few settlements away from Riverhome has been confirmed as a victim.
- **Konoha investigation**: Konoha is searching for the perpetrators. Riverhome does not know their identities, organisation, or location.
- **Forest holding site**: Ashe discovered an active prisoner-holding site in the surrounding forest. The captors she encountered were neutralised, but the prisoners and any wider network remain unresolved.

## Unresolved Mysteries
- **Trafficking Network**: Active regional operation, structure and leadership remain unknown.
- **Three-Line Symbol**: Recurring mark associated with the network. Confirmed again at the newly discovered forest holding site.
- **Trafficking Ledger & River Crossing**: Clues to wider operations and prisoner movement routes.
- **Forest Holding Site**: Exact prisoner count, destination of the prisoners, wider personnel, reinforcements, and connection between the site and the recent regional attacks remain unknown.

## Known Recent Facts
- Ashe infiltrated the forest holding site alone while invisible.
- She observed multiple armed captors, prisoners in cages, horses, and a wagon.
- The captors were preparing to move prisoners before dawn.
- Ashe attacked at maximum Lightspeed Slashes output and rapidly neutralised the captors she encountered.
- Ashe withdrew immediately after the assault.
- Prisoners remained at the site.
- Ashe has chosen to return with Hiro, her father, and a small team.

## Riverhome Emergency Preparations
- A concealed refuge has been built beneath the Old House for children and injured or otherwise vulnerable residents.
- Riverhome has increased readiness in response to the regional attacks.
- The settlement is preparing for defence without treating the rumours as proof that Riverhome itself will be attacked.
