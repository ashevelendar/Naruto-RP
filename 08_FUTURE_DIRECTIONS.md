# FUTURE DIRECTIONS

## Purpose
This file contains **player-provided meta knowledge about the intended direction of Ashe's journey**. It is not a prophecy, mandatory plot, or schedule. It should guide what kinds of opportunities may naturally exist in the world without forcing Ashe toward them.

## Ashe's Planned Exploration Direction
Ashe intends to leave the Land of Fire and travel through other lands and regions of the Naruto world.

A major long-term interest is eventually discovering and entering the less lawful side of the shinobi world, including things such as:
- Missing-nin
- Criminal shinobi
- Assassins and killers for hire
- Bounty hunters
- Underground fighting rings
- Illegal or semi-legal combat organisations
- Black-market contacts and services
- Mercenary work
- Criminal networks
- Other forms of the shinobi world's underworld

## GM Guidance
- **Do not push Ashe into this immediately.**
- Do not manufacture a criminal encounter simply because this file exists.
- Let travel, geography, rumours, chance encounters, local economies, relationships, and Ashe's own choices naturally create opportunities to discover this side of the world.
- Ashe may ignore, reject, leave, cooperate with, investigate, or become involved with underworld opportunities as she chooses.
- Different lands should have their own cultures, politics, dangers, shinobi traditions, criminal elements, and attitudes toward outsiders.
- The underworld should not be treated as a single unified organisation. It should be fragmented, competitive, dangerous, and populated by people with independent motives.
- Missing-nin should not all be villains. Some may be criminals, some mercenaries, some fugitives, some political refugees, and some simply people who abandoned their villages.
- Fighting rings, bounty work, and criminal opportunities should have meaningful risks and rewards rather than existing solely to give Ashe fights.
- Ashe's reputation may eventually develop within these circles based on her choices and actions, but this should emerge organically.
- Kōton remains absolutely secret. The existence and nature of Ashe's unique kekkei genkai must not become common knowledge merely because she enters the underworld.

## Continuity Rules for Future Travel
- Ashe was already a trained shinobi before beginning her journey. New villages and teachers should provide new perspectives, specialisations and refinements, not repeatedly teach her basic shinobi skills from scratch.
- Ashe already knew conventional swordsmanship before Konoha. Konoha's swordsman provided additional techniques and perspectives.
- Ashe already has private experience using Flash Step over moving water at high speed. Public Waterfalls training did not teach her Flash Step and did not involve Kōton.
- Ashe does not casually demonstrate secret Kōton abilities around other shinobi. The player must deliberately choose when the secrecy risk is accepted.
- Kōton-related experimentation should normally happen privately or under circumstances Ashe has deliberately chosen for secrecy.

## Intended Long-Term Feel
The journey should gradually broaden from:
**Riverhome -> Land of Fire -> other lands -> wider shinobi world -> underworld exposure**

The progression should feel like exploration rather than a quest chain. Ashe should encounter ordinary life, settlements, cultures, trade, shinobi, travellers, conflicts, opportunities, and quiet periods alongside more dangerous material.

This file is a directional preference, not an instruction to force a plot.
