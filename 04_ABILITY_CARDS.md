# ABILITY CARDS: KŌTON

* **Kōton Nature**: Unique Kekkei Genkai light manipulation used exclusively by Ashe Tsu. Light Control: **18/20**.
* **Absolute Secrecy**: Only Ashe's mother and father know Kōton exists. No one else knows its name, true nature, techniques, or mechanics unless Ashe explicitly reveals it.
* **Hard-Light Weapons**: Solid light constructs including blades, kunai, shuriken and other weapon shapes.
* **Selective Solidity**: Ashe can deliberately make only portions of a hard-light construct solid, or release solidity before/after contact. A construct can pass through an object while non-solid and become solid at a chosen point.
* **Dynamic Expansion / Geometry**: A hard-light construct can change its shape and size after formation. Ashe has successfully created a narrow blade, inserted it into a wooden training post, then expanded the construct while partially embedded. This is established as a Kōton capability, but living-target testing has not occurred.
* **Remote Hard-Light Formation**: Experimental. Ashe can form a small hard-light construct a short distance away using existing ambient or artificial light rather than forming it directly in her hand. Private testing established useful formation at roughly 3 metres, significantly more difficult formation around 5 metres, and only tiny short-lived constructs around 10 metres. Small constructs are substantially easier than complex or large ones. Moving or unstable light sources increase concentration demands.
* **Invisibility**: Manipulates ambient light to hide visually; does not stop non-visual tracking. Mixed/diffuse lighting is easier to maintain than concentrated direct light sources.
* **Invisible Hard-Light Constructs**: Newly established experimental application. Ashe can shift the visible-light signature of a hard-light weapon outside ordinary human visual perception while retaining physical solidity. Tested successfully with kunai, shuriken, swords, spears, staffs and other simple weapon geometries against inanimate targets. More complex shapes require more concentration. Specialised sensory abilities and environmental detection remain untested.
* **Invisible Selective-Solid Weapons**: Newly established experimental application. An invisible weapon does not need to remain hard while being positioned or moved. Ashe can maintain a long weapon as manipulated, non-solid light, then selectively harden only the relevant section at the intended point of interaction and release solidity immediately afterward. This substantially reduces the cost of maintaining long invisible weapons and combines naturally with Dynamic Expansion / Selective Solidity.
* **Lightspeed Slashes**: Extreme fraction-of-a-second speed burst combined with combat movement. Ashe can push the technique to very high chakra output for a much more aggressive burst of movement and chained slashes, but this causes severe chakra depletion.
* **Flash Step / Flash Jump**: Rapid movement/elevation shifts using light. Ashe has already used Flash Step over moving water at high speed and trained specifically to compensate for changing water movement while moving rapidly. Water and unstable terrain are therefore not entirely unfamiliar to her Flash Step movement.
* **Focused Light vs. Distributed Focus**: Concentrating light to heat/cut metals versus spreading energy through constructs to prevent collapse.
* **Radiant Edge**: Advanced hard-light cutting technique that rapidly cuts through steel using Distributed Focus.
* **Eclipse**: Manipulates light entering an opponent's eyes (Partial, Full, Pulse, Moving, Dual). Year 3 refinement allows minutes-long duration with low chakra costs.
* **Remote Light Redirection**: Bends existing light paths to see around obstacles or view alternate angles without visual line-of-sight. Urban environments with many artificial light sources provide useful experimental conditions.

## Kōton Development Principles
- Kōton constructs do not necessarily need to originate from Ashe's body or immediate vicinity.
- Ashe is increasingly treating light as a manipulable medium rather than merely a source for handheld constructs.
- Refinement currently focuses on precision, geometry, timing, efficiency, and environmental interaction rather than simply increasing raw power.
- Complex transformations and remote formation increase cognitive and chakra demands.
- Hard-light solidity is significantly more demanding than manipulating light without making it physically solid. Ashe can exploit this by keeping a weapon non-solid while positioning it and only hardening it at the moment of intended interaction.

## Recent Controlled Experiments
- Ashe trained with a Konoha swordsman and studied additional sword footwork, recovery, body alignment, timing, and weapon commitment. Swordsmanship itself predates Konoha.
- She applied those observations to her existing hard-light sword use.
- She discovered that selective solidity can reduce the impact normally transmitted through a physical blade.
- She experimented with dynamic expansion of a hard-light blade inside a wooden training post, confirming that construct geometry can be altered after penetration.
- She tested remote formation from a candle and other ambient light sources in her Konoha inn room.
- She privately developed invisible hard-light weapons in the Land of Waterfalls and tested their physical performance against inanimate targets.
- She discovered that invisible weapons retain their physical cutting/penetrating properties while their visible-light signature is suppressed.
- She discovered that keeping an invisible weapon non-solid during movement, then selectively hardening it only at the point of interaction, greatly reduces the maintenance burden of long constructs.
- This latest principle is an extension of the already-established Dynamic Expansion and Selective Solidity concepts.
- Ashe has previously used Flash Step across moving water and learned to compensate for the changing surface at high movement speed. This is established experience, not a new discovery.
- All recent invisible-weapon experiments have been private and unwitnessed by other shinobi.
