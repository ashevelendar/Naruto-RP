# ABILITY CARDS: KŌTON

* **Kōton Nature**: Unique Kekkei Genkai light manipulation used exclusively by Ashe Tsu. Light Control: **18/20**.
* **Absolute Secrecy**: Only Ashe's mother and father know Kōton exists. No one else knows its name, true nature, techniques, or mechanics unless Ashe explicitly reveals it.
* **Hard-Light Weapons**: Solid light constructs including blades, kunai, shuriken and other weapon shapes.
* **Selective Solidity**: Ashe can deliberately make only portions of a hard-light construct solid, or release solidity before/after contact. A construct can pass through an object while non-solid and become solid at a chosen point.
* **Dynamic Expansion / Geometry**: A hard-light construct can change its shape and size after formation. Ashe has successfully created a narrow blade, inserted it into a wooden training post, then expanded the construct while partially embedded. This is established as a Kōton capability.
* **Internal / Embedded Hardening**: Established during the Konoha experiments. A non-solid construct can occupy an internal position within an inanimate target and then become solid while already embedded. Ashe does not need to slash the surface of a target. Correct positioning and timing of hardening are sufficient for the principle to work.
* **Remote Hard-Light Formation**: Experimental. Ashe can form a small hard-light construct a short distance away using existing ambient or artificial light rather than forming it directly in her hand. Private testing established useful formation at roughly 3 metres, significantly more difficult formation around 5 metres, and only tiny short-lived constructs around 10 metres. Small constructs are substantially easier than complex or large ones. Moving or unstable light sources increase concentration demands.
* **Invisibility**: Manipulates ambient light to hide visually; does not stop non-visual tracking. Mixed/diffuse lighting is easier to maintain than concentrated direct light sources.
* **Invisible Hard-Light Constructs**: Established experimental application. Ashe can shift the visible-light signature of a hard-light weapon outside ordinary human visual perception while retaining physical properties. Tested successfully with kunai, shuriken, swords, spears, staffs and other simple weapon geometries against inanimate targets. More complex shapes require more concentration. Specialised sensory abilities and environmental detection remain untested.
* **Invisible Selective-Solid Weapons**: Refined experimental application. An invisible weapon does not need to remain hard while being positioned or moved. Ashe can maintain a long weapon as manipulated, non-solid light, then selectively harden only the relevant section at the intended point of interaction and release solidity immediately afterward. This substantially reduces the cost of maintaining long invisible weapons and combines naturally with Dynamic Expansion and Internal / Embedded Hardening.
* **Invisible Dynamic Expansion**: Experimental combination of established principles. An invisible non-solid construct can be positioned and its geometry altered before selected portions are hardened.
* **Lightspeed Slashes**: Extreme fraction-of-a-second speed burst combined with combat movement. Ashe can push the technique to very high chakra output for a much more aggressive burst of movement and chained slashes, but this causes severe chakra depletion.
* **Flash Step / Flash Jump**: Rapid movement/elevation shifts using light. Ashe has already used Flash Step over moving water at high speed and trained specifically to compensate for changing water movement while moving rapidly.
* **High-Output Flash Step**: Newly established advanced application. Ashe can push Flash Step beyond her normal operating level for an extremely short burst, fast enough to cross a waterfall with almost no water contact. It requires substantially more chakra and causes greater physical strain than ordinary Flash Step.
* **Continuous High-Output Flash Step**: Advanced experimental refinement. Ashe can maintain the extreme movement through a short route rather than treating each burst as an isolated point-to-point Flash Step. She can accelerate, alter trajectory while still moving, and accelerate again without fully resetting. The intended pattern is **A → B → C**, with B acting as a trajectory point rather than a stop. At short distances the movement can appear almost instantaneous to an observer despite being actual physical travel.
* **High-Speed Trajectory Control**: Ashe has trained herself to redirect at extreme speed without fully slowing to a conventional run. Sharp changes remain difficult and dangerous. Terrain knowledge is becoming increasingly important because the environment passes too quickly for normal reactive navigation at maximum output.
* **Blind High-Output Movement, Training Goal**: Ashe is beginning a six-month programme to make continuous High-Output Flash Step reliable without sight in familiar terrain. This is not yet mastered. In known terrain she intends to navigate by memorised spatial knowledge; in unfamiliar terrain she intends to use vision to process the environment at extreme speed.
* **High-Speed Perception, Training Goal**: Ashe will train her visual processing and reaction ability at High-Output Flash Step speeds, with the goal of clearly perceiving terrain, moving objects and threats rather than experiencing them only as blur.
* **High-Speed Projectile Control, Training Goal**: Ashe intends to learn to create and throw hard-light kunai or shuriken at specific moving targets while she herself is moving at extreme Flash Step speed. This is not yet mastered.
* **Focused Light vs. Distributed Focus**: Concentrating light to heat/cut metals versus spreading energy through constructs to prevent collapse.
* **Radiant Edge**: Advanced hard-light cutting technique that rapidly cuts through steel using Distributed Focus.
* **Eclipse**: Manipulates light entering an opponent's eyes (Partial, Full, Pulse, Moving, Dual). Year 3 refinement allows minutes-long duration with low chakra costs.
* **Remote Light Redirection**: Bends existing light paths to see around obstacles or view alternate angles without visual line-of-sight. Urban environments with many artificial light sources provide useful experimental conditions.

## Kōton Development Principles
- Kōton constructs do not necessarily need to originate from Ashe's body or immediate vicinity.
- Ashe is increasingly treating light as a manipulable medium rather than merely a source for handheld constructs.
- Refinement currently focuses on precision, geometry, timing, efficiency, perception and environmental interaction rather than simply increasing raw power.
- Complex transformations and remote formation increase cognitive and chakra demands.
- Hard-light solidity is significantly more demanding than manipulating light without making it physically solid. Ashe can exploit this by keeping a weapon non-solid while positioning it and only hardening it at the moment of intended interaction.
- Existing techniques and principles should be combined and refined rather than treated as newly discovered each time Ashe finds a new application.

## Recent Controlled Experiments
- Ashe trained with a Konoha swordsman and studied additional sword footwork, recovery, body alignment, timing, and weapon commitment. Swordsmanship itself predates Konoha.
- She applied those observations to her existing hard-light sword use.
- She discovered that selective solidity can reduce the impact normally transmitted through a physical blade.
- She experimented with dynamic expansion of a hard-light blade inside a wooden training post, confirming that construct geometry can be altered after penetration.
- She tested remote formation from a candle and other ambient light sources in her Konoha inn room.
- She privately developed invisible hard-light weapons in the Land of Waterfalls and tested their physical performance against inanimate targets.
- She discovered that invisible weapons retain their physical cutting/penetrating properties while their visible-light signature is suppressed.
- She discovered that keeping an invisible weapon non-solid during movement, then selectively hardening it only at the point of interaction, greatly reduces the maintenance burden of long constructs.
- This latest principle is an extension of the already-established Dynamic Expansion, Selective Solidity, and Internal / Embedded Hardening concepts.
- Ashe has previously used Flash Step across moving water and learned to compensate for the changing surface at high movement speed. This is established experience, not a new discovery.
- After the initial week of refinement, Ashe spent approximately another week hunting animals for food while using the new principles. The animals served as practical living targets because they move, jump, flee and attack, allowing Ashe to practise positioning, timing and selective hardening against moving targets while simultaneously obtaining food.
- The animal experiments included different simple weapon geometries and repeated use of invisible, non-solid positioning followed by selective hardening. They provided the first practical moving-living-target feedback for the new application.
- Ashe then developed High-Output Flash Step by using the Land of Waterfalls environment, successfully crossing a waterfall almost completely dry.
- She refined the burst into continuous movement, learning to redirect at extreme speed rather than stopping between points. A successful A → B → C route was established with B functioning as a trajectory point rather than a stop.
- The next six months are dedicated to refining this movement, training blindfolded navigation in familiar terrain, improving perception at extreme speed, and eventually throwing hard-light kunai/shuriken accurately at moving targets while travelling at extreme speed.
- These six-month goals are active training objectives and must not be treated as completed abilities until earned through play.
- All recent invisible-weapon, animal-hunting and High-Output Flash Step experiments have been private and unwitnessed by other shinobi.
