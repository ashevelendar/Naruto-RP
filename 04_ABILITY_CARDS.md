# ABILITY CARDS: KŌTON

* **Kōton Nature**: Unique Kekkei Genkai light manipulation used exclusively by Ashe Tsu[cite: 1]. Light Control: **18/20**[cite: 1].
* **Hard-Light Weapons**: Solid light constructs (blades, kunai, shuriken)[cite: 1].
* **Invisibility**: Manipulates ambient light to hide visually; does not stop non-visual tracking[cite: 1].
* **Lightspeed Slashes**: Extreme fraction-of-a-second speed burst combined with combat movement[cite: 1].
* **Flash Step / Flash Jump**: Rapid movement/elevation shifts using light[cite: 1].
* **Focused Light vs. Distributed Focus**: Concentrating light to heat/cut metals versus spreading energy through constructs to prevent collapse[cite: 1].
* **Radiant Edge**: Advanced hard-light cutting technique that rapidly cuts through steel using Distributed Focus[cite: 1].
* **Eclipse**: Manipulates light entering an opponent's eyes (Partial, Full, Pulse, Moving, Dual)[cite: 1]. Year 3 refinement allows minutes-long duration with low chakra costs[cite: 1].
* **Remote Light Redirection**: Bends existing light paths to see around obstacles or view alternate angles without visual line-of-sight[cite: 1].
