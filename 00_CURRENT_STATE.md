- Konoha did **not** teach Ashe swordsmanship from scratch. Her Konoha training provided additional observations, techniques, tactical perspectives, and exposure to other fighting styles.

- Ashe already has established experience using Flash Step across moving water at high speed and compensating for the changing surface. This was learned before the current Waterfalls training and must not be treated as a new ability or something she casually demonstrates publicly.

## Kōton Recent Development
- Hard-Light Weapons: refined through conventional swordsmanship training.
- Selective Solidity: established. Portions of constructs can be hardened or de-hardened independently.
- Dynamic Expansion / Geometry: established experimentally on wooden training material. A narrow blade can be inserted and expanded while partially embedded. Practical use against living targets remains untested.
- Remote Hard-Light Formation: experimental. Ashe can form a small hard-light construct a short distance away by using existing ambient or artificial light rather than forming it directly in her hand. Current range, speed, chakra cost and combat reliability are unknown.
- Urban light manipulation: Remote Light Redirection and Invisibility have been tested privately in Konoha. Concentrated artificial light can make Invisibility more difficult than diffuse or mixed lighting.
- Kōton was used during the Chūnin Exam invasion while Ashe was masked. Several shinobi may have witnessed unusual light constructs, but no one has established Kōton's true identity or linked it to Ashe.
- Remote Hard-Light Formation has **not** been deliberately demonstrated to the Waterfalls shinobi Ashe trained with.
