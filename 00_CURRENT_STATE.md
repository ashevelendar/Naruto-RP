## Kōton Current Development
- Hard-Light Weapons: established, including blades, kunai, shuriken and other simple forms.
- Selective Solidity: established and highly refined.
- Dynamic Expansion / Geometry: established. Ashe can change a construct's shape and size after formation. In Konoha she created a narrow hard-light blade, inserted it into a wooden training post, then expanded the construct while partially embedded.
- Internal/Embedded Hardening: established from the Konoha experiments and now deliberately integrated into invisible weapons. A construct can pass through an object while non-solid, occupy an internal position, then become solid while already inside the object.
- Remote Hard-Light Formation: experimental. Small constructs can be formed a short distance away using ambient/artificial light. Useful formation has been achieved around 3 metres, with sharply increasing difficulty at greater distances.
- Invisible Hard-Light Constructs: established experimental capability. Ashe can suppress the visible-light signature of a construct beyond ordinary human visual perception while retaining physical properties.
- Invisible Selective-Solidity: refined. Ashe can maintain long invisible constructs in a non-solid state, then harden only the relevant section at the intended moment of interaction. This is considerably cheaper than maintaining continuous hard-light solidity.
- Invisible Dynamic Expansion: experimental combination of established principles.
- Invisibility: manipulates ambient light to hide Ashe visually; does not stop non-visual tracking.
- Eclipse: manipulates light entering an opponent's eyes. Year 3 refinement allows minutes-long duration with low chakra costs, including partial and moving interference.
- Remote Light Redirection: bends existing light paths to see around obstacles or view alternate angles.
- Lightspeed Slashes: extreme fraction-of-a-second speed burst combined with combat movement. Very high chakra output causes severe depletion.

## Flash Step Development
- Flash Step / Flash Jump: rapid movement/elevation shifts using light.
- Established prior experience: Ashe can use Flash Step over moving water at high speed and compensate for changing water movement.
- **High-Output Flash Step:** Ashe has successfully increased Flash Step output beyond her ordinary operating level, crossing a waterfall almost completely dry.
- The higher-output version is an extremely short-duration burst, substantially faster than ordinary Flash Step, with high chakra expenditure and physical strain.
- Ashe has now refined it into **continuous controlled high-speed movement** rather than separate point-to-point bursts.
- The intended movement pattern is **A → B → C**, where B is a trajectory point rather than a stop. Ashe can accelerate to extreme speed, alter trajectory while still moving, and accelerate again without fully resetting the technique.
- From an observer's perspective, short-distance movement can appear almost instantaneous, effectively resembling teleportation despite Ashe physically travelling through the intervening space.
- Current training goal: make this continuous movement reliable enough to execute blindfolded in terrain Ashe knows well. She should not need to see a familiar route to navigate it at extreme speed.
- When Ashe can see an unfamiliar route and its terrain, the intended goal is even greater speed because she can use real-time visual information rather than relying on memorised spatial knowledge.
- This distinction is an active training objective, not a completed mastery.
