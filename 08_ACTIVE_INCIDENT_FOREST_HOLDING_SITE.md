# ACTIVE INCIDENT: FOREST HOLDING SITE

## Status
**Active / unresolved**

## Date
Year 3, Early Spring

## Discovery
Ashe investigated a suspicious structure in the forest alone after reports of bandits, missing-nin, and attacks on small settlements began reaching Riverhome.

She approached while using Kōton Invisibility, using the surrounding terrain and darkness to avoid visual detection.

She also used **Remote Light Redirection** to inspect the interior from an alternate visual angle without exposing herself at the window.

## What Ashe Confirmed
- The structure was an active holding site.
- Multiple armed captors were present.
- Prisoners were being held in cages.
- A prisoner referred to as "the girl" was among those being discussed.
- The occupants were preparing to move prisoners before dawn.
- Horses and a wagon were present.
- The recurring three-line symbol associated with the trafficking network was marked near the structure.

## Initial Assault
Ashe chose not to wait for the captors to move the prisoners.

She entered alone while invisible and used **Lightspeed Slashes** at maximum practical output, chaining rapid movement and attacks through the building before withdrawing immediately.

The captors she encountered were neutralised.

The assault was deliberately brief. Ashe did not remain to investigate the site after the attack because her chakra reserves had been heavily depleted.

## Current Situation
- Prisoners remained inside when Ashe withdrew.
- The site has not yet been fully searched.
- The exact number of prisoners is unknown.
- The exact number of captors is unknown beyond those Ashe encountered.
- Reinforcements, additional network members, and nearby allied sites are unknown.
- The relationship between this holding site and the recent attacks on surrounding settlements is unconfirmed.

## Ashe's Current Decision
Ashe has returned to Riverhome and intends to return to the holding site with:
- Hiro
- Her father
- A small team

The team is being brought primarily to secure the site, protect and extract the prisoners, and investigate safely after Ashe's initial high-output assault.

## Knowledge Boundaries
### Ashe Knows
- The location of the site.
- The site contains prisoners.
- Captors were operating there.
- The three-line symbol was present.
- Prisoners were scheduled to move before dawn at the time of discovery.
- She neutralised the captors she encountered.

### Ashe Does Not Know
- Who leads the network.
- Where the prisoners were ultimately intended to go.
- How many people are involved in the wider operation.
- Whether the recent regional attacks are connected.
- Whether the site has additional hidden rooms, tunnels, records, or personnel.
- Whether anyone knows Ashe has discovered it.

### Do Not Assume
The forest holding site is associated with the trafficking network because of the symbol and circumstances, but the broader regional bandit/missing-nin attacks must not be treated as the same organisation without evidence.
