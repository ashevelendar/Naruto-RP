# HISTORICAL TIMELINE

## Year 0: The Journey Begins
- Ashe leaves home at 16 to explore.
- Meets travelers Renga and Toma; learns to enjoy solitary travel.
- Investigates an abandoned wagon, discovers a trafficking network, destroys a holding site, and rescues 9 civilians.
- Founds **Riverhome** (Day 50, initial population: 10).
- Establishes first council and 10 foundational laws.
- Brief contact and sparring draw with Konoha **Genin** Ren. *(Corrected from the earlier typo "Genkin".)*

## Year 1: Building Riverhome
- Population grows to 17–20.
- Constructed central stone-lined well, 7 houses, forge, workshop, and trade routes.
- Introduced adult apprenticeship system and Riverhome Guard.
- Ashe discovers **The Hollow**.
- Kōton breakthroughs: *Focused Light*, *Distributed Focus*, *Radiant Edge*.

## Year 2: Consolidation & Family
- Schoolhouse built; Riverhome Charter established (communal ownership).
- Ashe's parents arrive and decide to stay permanently.
- Father becomes Guard Academy Combat Instructor; Mother takes over Civil Affairs on the Council.
- The Hollow opened to parents; family begins joint training.
- Kōton breakthroughs: *Eclipse* and *Remote Light Redirection*.
- Ashe spends the year refining Eclipse, reducing its chakra cost, extending its useful duration to minutes, developing Partial Eclipse, and learning to maintain Eclipse while moving.
- Riverhome grows to 51 people by the following spring: 40 adults and 11 children.

## Year 3: Early Spring, Present Day
- Riverhome is a stable, self-sustaining regional hub with established trade, agriculture, livestock, school, Guard, and Guard Academy.
- Ashe's Kōton control reaches **18/20**, with refinement increasingly focused on efficiency, reliability, movement, and tactical application rather than constant invention.
- Regional rumours begin circulating about bandits, missing-nin, and other armed groups attacking small settlements.
- A settlement a few villages away from Riverhome is confirmed to have been attacked. Konoha is searching for the perpetrators, but Riverhome has no confirmed information about their identity or location.
- Riverhome establishes a concealed emergency refuge beneath the Old House for children and injured or otherwise vulnerable residents.
- Ashe places the settlement on heightened readiness while continuing to treat the regional threat as uncertain rather than assuming an attack is inevitable.
- Ashe investigates a suspicious forest structure alone using Invisibility and Remote Light Redirection.
- The structure is confirmed as a holding site containing armed captors and prisoners. The recurring three-line symbol associated with the trafficking network is found there.
- Ashe learns that prisoners are due to be moved before dawn.
- Ashe uses maximum-output Lightspeed Slashes to rapidly neutralise the captors she encounters, then immediately withdraws from the site.
- Prisoners remain at the holding site.
- Ashe returns to Riverhome and decides to go back with Hiro, her father, and a small team to secure the site and deal with the prisoners.
