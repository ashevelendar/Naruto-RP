# HISTORICAL TIMELINE

- Ashe leaves home at 16 to explore[cite: 1].
- Meets travelers Renga and Toma; learns to enjoy solitary travel[cite: 1].
- Investigates an abandoned wagon, discovers a trafficking network, destroys a holding site, and rescues 9 civilians[cite: 1].
- Founds **Riverhome** (Day 50, initial population: 10)[cite: 1].

## Year 1: Building Riverhome
- Population grows to 17–20[cite: 1].

- Introduced adult apprenticeship system and Riverhome Guard[cite: 1].
- Ashe discovers **The Hollow**[cite: 1].
- Kōton breakthroughs: *Focused Light*, *Distributed Focus*, *Radiant Edge*[cite: 1].

## Year 2: Consolidation & Family

- The Hollow opened to parents; family begins joint training[cite: 1].
- Kōton breakthroughs: *Eclipse* and *Remote Light Redirection*[cite: 1].

## Year 3: Present Day
- Population reaches **51** (40 adults, 11 children)[cite: 1].
- Riverhome is a self-sustaining regional hub[cite: 1].
