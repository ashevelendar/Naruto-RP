# LOCATION CARDS

## Riverhome
- **Type**: Small free settlement in the Land of Fire[cite: 1].
- **Population**: 51 (40 adults, 11 children)[cite: 1].
- **Government**: Free, council-governed settlement led by First Speaker Ashe Tsu[cite: 1].
- **Character**: Practical, growing, self-sufficient; people deliberately travel here now[cite: 1].

## Riverhome Council
Manages ordinary settlement affairs[cite: 1]. Ashe focuses on external affairs, emergencies, and long-term direction[cite: 1]. Her mother handles internal administration, civil disputes, and welfare[cite: 1].

## Guard Academy
Led by Hiro (Head) and Ashe's father (Specialist)[cite: 1]. 12 regular trainees[cite: 1]. Focuses on practical defense, evacuation, survival, and knowing when not to fight[cite: 1]. Not a shinobi academy[cite: 1].

## Schoolhouse
Active and expanded for 11 children[cite: 1]. Focused on proper community education rather than disguised labor[cite: 1].

## The Hollow
- **Type**: Secret underground Kōton training/experimentation facility[cite: 1].
- **Known Only To**: Ashe, Ashe's father, Ashe's mother[cite: 1].
- **Key Testing**: Hard-light weapons, Focused Light, Distributed Focus, Radiant Edge, Eclipse variants, Remote Light Redirection[cite: 1].
