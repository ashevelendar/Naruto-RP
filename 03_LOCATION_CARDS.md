# LOCATION CARDS

## Riverhome
- **Type**: Small free settlement in the Land of Fire.
- **Population**: 51 (40 adults, 11 children).
- **Government**: Free, council-governed settlement led by First Speaker Ashe Tsu.
- **Character**: Practical, growing, self-sufficient; people deliberately travel here now.
- **Current security posture**: Elevated due to reports of attacks on nearby small settlements and the discovery of an active holding site in the surrounding forest.

## Riverhome Council
Manages ordinary settlement affairs. Ashe focuses on external affairs, emergencies, and long-term direction. Her mother handles internal administration, civil disputes, and welfare.

## Guard Academy
Led by Hiro (Head) and Ashe's father (Specialist). 12 regular trainees. Focuses on practical defense, evacuation, survival, and knowing when not to fight. Not a shinobi academy.

## Schoolhouse
Active and expanded for 11 children. Focused on proper community education rather than disguised labour.

## Old House
The original dwelling Ashe found when Riverhome was founded. It is no longer a shack and now forms part of the established settlement.

### Emergency Refuge
A concealed refuge has been constructed beneath the Old House. It is intended primarily for children and injured or otherwise vulnerable residents during an attack. The existence and purpose of the refuge are known within Riverhome as part of its emergency preparations.

## The Hollow
- **Type**: Secret underground Kōton training/experimentation facility.
- **Known Only To**: Ashe, Ashe's father, Ashe's mother.
- **Key Testing**: Hard-light weapons, Focused Light, Distributed Focus, Radiant Edge, Eclipse variants, Remote Light Redirection.

## Forest Holding Site
- **Status**: Active incident location, not yet fully secured.
- **Location**: In the forest within travelling distance of Riverhome; exact location is known to Ashe and, after her return, will be shared with the small team she takes there.
- **Nature**: Hidden holding site used by armed captors to imprison people.
- **Evidence**: Recurring three-line symbol associated with the trafficking network was found at the site.
- **Observed features**: Main structure, lantern-lit interior, horses, wagon, multiple armed occupants, and prisoner cages.
- **Current situation**: Ashe neutralised the captors she encountered and withdrew. Prisoners remained inside. Ashe is returning with Hiro, her father, and a small team.
